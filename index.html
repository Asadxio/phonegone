⚠️
Crucial TRAI 24-Hour Rule: When you get a duplicate SIM from your telecom operator, TRAI security regulations block SMS services for 24 hours. You must wait 24 hours before the CEIR OTP can be received on that duplicate SIM.
How to find your IMEI without having your phone
  • Android devices: Go to google.com/android/find, sign in, and click the (i) info icon next to your device model to view its IMEI.
  • Apple devices: Go to appleid.apple.com, sign in, click Devices, and select your iPhone to reveal its IMEI.
  • Phone Retail Box: Check the white barcode label on the back or bottom of the original device packaging.
  • Purchase Invoice: Check your receipt from Amazon, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, or your local retailer where the IMEI is printed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common doubts about lost phones in India.

Can PhoneGone track or find my phone?
No. PhoneGone is strictly an informational rescue guide. Only official device locators (Google Find My Device for Android, Apple iCloud Find for iPhone) or authorized law enforcement agencies using telecom tower data can track a device. Never pay third-party websites claiming to track your phone via IMEI number; those are scams.
What is CEIR and does it really block the phone?
Yes. The Central Equipment Identity Register (CEIR) is an official initiative by the Department of Telecommunications (DoT), Government of India. When you submit a valid blocking request with a police report, the device's IMEI is blacklisted across all Indian telecom networks (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL). Even if a thief changes the SIM card, the handset will not connect to any cellular network, and a police trace alert is generated.
Why do I need a duplicate SIM before CEIR, and why is the OTP delayed?
The official CEIR portal sends a verification OTP to the exact mobile number that was lost in the handset. Because you no longer have the original SIM, you must get a duplicate SIM from your telecom operator. Under TRAI cybersecurity regulations, incoming and outgoing SMS is blocked for 24 hours on newly swapped SIM cards to prevent SIM swap fraud. You must wait 24 hours after SIM activation before you can receive the CEIR OTP.
Should I file an FIR or a Lost Article Report?
If your phone was stolen, pickpocketed, or snatched, that is a cognizable criminal offense and you should file an FIR (First Information Report) at the local police station or via your State Police e-FIR portal. If you simply misplaced or left your phone somewhere, many state police portals provide a "Lost Property / Missing Article Report" service. The CEIR portal accepts both valid police FIRs and online Lost Article complaints.
How do I prevent thieves from misusing Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm?
In India, UPI requires device binding via your SIM card. Step 1: Immediately call your telecom operator to block your SIM; once the SIM is deactivated, UPI cannot function. Step 2: Call your bank's 24x7 helpline to temporarily freeze UPI access and net banking. Step 3: Contact payment app helplines (Paytm: 0120-3888388, PhonePe: 080-68727374) to temporarily deactivate your account.
If I remotely erase my iPhone, can the thief use it?
No, as long as "Find My" was enabled before the loss. When you remotely erase an iPhone, "Activation Lock" remains active. The phone cannot be set up or reactivated without your Apple Account password. Crucial warning: When erasing, DO NOT choose "Remove from Account"; doing so removes Activation Lock and gives the thief a usable phone.
How do I unblock my phone if it is recovered?
Visit the official CEIR Unblock Request form (ceir.sancharsaathi.gov.in/Request/CeirUserUnblockRequestDirect.jsp). Enter the 18-digit Request ID you received when blocking, the mobile number you provided, the reason for unblocking (e.g., "Found by self" or "Recovered by police"), and the OTP received on your mobile. Once approved, the IMEI will be restored across telecom networks.

No. PhoneGone is completely client-side. There is no user database, no tracking cookies, no advertising scripts, and no backend server receiving your data. Your checklist progress is stored solely in your current browser's localStorage and can be wiped instantly by clicking "Reset Progress".
